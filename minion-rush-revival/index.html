How toinstall

Android
  1. On your phone, download the APK for the version you want.
  2. Open it. When asked, allow installing from that app, then tap Install.
iPhone / iPad
  1. Install Sideloadly on your computer.
  2. Plug in your phone, drag the IPA into Sideloadly, sign in with your Apple ID and press Start.
  3. On your phone, trust your Apple ID in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.
  4. On iOS 16 or newer, turn on Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode.

With a free Apple ID the app stops opening after 7 days. Sideload it again and your save stays.
